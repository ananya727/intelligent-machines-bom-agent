The diagram shows a fruit-and-cheese plate assembly cell with an upper ingredient/loading area, a shared robotic manipulation zone, and two parallel rows of trays in the lower assembly/inspection area. Operators O1 and O2 load the upper area; operators O3 and O4 are associated with the finished-product end. Inputs are three cheese varieties, grapes, apples, wrapped crackers, empty plastic trays, and separate clear lids. Trays are dispensed individually, filled, inspected by camera, closed with lids, and passed to finished-product staging. Inspection or closure failures go to reject/rework.
